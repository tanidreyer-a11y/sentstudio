## Common Mistakes When Choosing & Wearing Fragrances in South Africa

To ensure your chosen scent truly shines, especially in our unique climate, avoid these pitfalls:

*   **Over-spraying light scents in hot weather:** More isn't always better. Our oil-based 'Inspired by Baccarat Rouge 540' offers excellent projection on its own.
*   **Ignoring skin hydration:** Perfume clings better to moisturized skin. Apply a fragrance-free lotion before your scent.
*   **Rubbing wrists together:** This breaks down the fragrance molecules and alters the scent's intended progression.
*   **Buying purely on brand name:** Focus on scent profile and longevity. Our 'Inspired by Dior Sauvage' or 'Inspired by YSL Black Opium' offer the same luxury feel without the designer price.
*   **Not testing on skin:** Always test a fragrance on your skin, as body chemistry can significantly alter how a scent smells.
