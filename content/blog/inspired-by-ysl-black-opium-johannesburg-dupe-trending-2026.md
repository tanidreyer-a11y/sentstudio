## Common Mistakes When Choosing and Wearing Inspired Fragrances

While the world of inspired fragrances offers incredible value, a few pitfalls can diminish your experience. Avoid these common mistakes to ensure you get the most out of your Scent Studio 'Inspired by Black Opium':

*   **Expecting Identical Packaging:** Focus on the scent, not the bottle. Inspired fragrances prioritise the perfume itself, allowing for the significant cost savings that make them so appealing. The beauty is in the oil, not the elaborate designer flask.
*   **Not Testing on Skin:** Perfumes react uniquely with individual body chemistry. Always test a small amount on your skin (e.g., wrist or inner elbow) at the store. Allow it to develop for at least 30 minutes to truly appreciate the dry-down – the notes that linger longest.
*   **Applying to Clothes Instead of Skin:** While spraying a fine mist on clothing can add a pleasant scent, oil-based perfumes are designed to meld with your skin's natural warmth, enhancing their longevity and sillage. They can also potentially stain delicate fabrics.
*   **Over-Applying:** Because oil-based perfumes are more concentrated, a little goes a long way. Start with a small dab or two on pulse points (wrists, neck, behind the ears). You can always add more, but you can't easily undo too much.
*   **Storing Incorrectly:** Heat, light, and humidity can degrade a perfume's quality. Store your 'Inspired by Black Opium' in a cool, dark place, away from direct sunlight and extreme temperature fluctuations, to preserve its integrity and extend its shelf life, crucial in the varying South African climate.
*   **Assuming All Dupes Are Equal:** The quality of inspired fragrances varies wildly.
