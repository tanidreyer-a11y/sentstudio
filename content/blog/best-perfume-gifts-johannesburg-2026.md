## Why oil-based perfume makes a better gift

Alcohol-based eau de parfums evaporate fast — beautiful for the first hour, gone by lunch. Oil-based perfumes sit on the skin, releasing slowly through the day. In Johannesburg's dry highveld air, that difference is the difference between *"what are you wearing?"* and *"did you put on perfume this morning?"*

A gift that lasts twelve hours feels generous. A gift that lasts two doesn't.

## Common gifting mistakes (and how to avoid them)

- **Buying the wrong concentration.** Body sprays are not perfume. Always check it's an oil or EDP.
- **Guessing florals for women, woods for men.** Half the women in Johannesburg wear Sauvage. Half the men wear Baccarat Rouge. Gender the bottle, not the scent.
- **Going by the bottle, not the juice.** A R3,000 designer bottle with a 3-hour wear time is not a better gift than a R450 oil that lasts all day.
- **Buying online from sellers you don't know.** Counterfeit perfume is rife in South Africa. Buy from a physical boutique you can walk into — like ours.
- **Forgetting the wrap.** We gift-wrap for free, in store, no extra charge.
