## Why choose an oil-based perfume as a gift?

Our fragrances are alcohol-free and oil-based. You apply them to your pulse points, and they sit on the skin instead of evaporating the way a spray does. That makes them gentle, and the bottles are easy to pop in a handbag or a stocking. We do not claim a set number of hours of wear, because skin, weather and how much you apply all change the result. For tips on making any scent last, see [our guide](/blog/why-your-perfume-fades-fast-south-africa).
