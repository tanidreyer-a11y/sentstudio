## Why winter fragrance is different

Cold air holds fragrance differently to summer heat. Light florals and aquatics evaporate fast and lose their character. Warmer, oilier compositions — orientals, gourmands, woody ambers — slow-release on the skin and project for hours. Because Scent Studio fragrances are **oil-based** rather than alcohol-heavy, they already favour this slow, intimate sillage. Pair that with the right notes and a single spray genuinely lasts the working day.

## Common mistakes Johannesburg shoppers make in winter

- **Over-spraying.** Oil-based fragrances are concentrated — two sprays at the pulse points beat six. Cold air carries scent further than you think.
- **Sticking to summer favourites out of habit.** A light fresh aquatic that worked in February will feel thin and short-lived in June. Rotate seasonally.
- **Ignoring the gourmand category.** Vanilla, coffee, caramel, tobacco — these are not "feminine" or "masculine", they are warm. Both our men's and women's customers wear Tobacco Vanille and Baccarat Rouge interchangeably.
- **Buying blind online without smelling.** If you can make it to Flora Centre, come test on skin. Highveld air changes how a fragrance opens.
